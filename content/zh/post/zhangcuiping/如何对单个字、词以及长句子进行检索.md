# 如何对单个字、词以及长句子进行检索？

1. 创建表并插入数据。

   ```
   Drop table if exists t_search;
   
   CREATE TABLE t_search (id int, title text, keyword text, abstract text, body text, ti tsvector);
   
   INSERT INTO t_search(id, title, keyword, abstract, body) VALUES (1, 'Northern area', 'Beijing', 'China','China, officially the People Republic of China (PRC), located in Asia, is the most populous state.');
   
   INSERT INTO t_search(id, title, keyword, abstract, body) VALUES (1, 'eastern area', 'Shanghai', 'China','China, located in east of China, is an international city.');
   ```

2. 对单个字进行全文检索。

   ```
   MogDB=# Select * from t_search where to_tsvector(keyword)@@ to_tsquery( 'Beijing' );
    id |     title     | keyword | abstract |                                                body
                 | ti
   ----+---------------+---------+----------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   --------------+----
     1 | Northern area | Beijing | China    | China, officially the People Republic of China (PRC), located in Asia, is the most po
   pulous state. |
   (1 row)
   ```

3. 对单个词进行全文检索。

   ```
   MogDB=# Select * from t_search where to_tsvector(title)@@ plainto_tsquery( 'eastern area' );
    id |    title     | keyword  | abstract |                            body                            | ti
   ----+--------------+----------+----------+------------------------------------------------------------+----
     1 | eastern area | Shanghai | China    | China, located in east of China, is an international city. |
   (1 row)
   ```

4. 对长句子进行全文检索。

   ```
   MogDB=# Select * from t_search where to_tsvector(body)@@ plainto_tsquery( 'China, located in east of China, is an international city.' );
    id |    title     | keyword  | abstract |                            body                            | ti
   ----+--------------+----------+----------+------------------------------------------------------------+----
     1 | eastern area | Shanghai | China    | China, located in east of China, is an international city. |
   (1 row)
   ```
